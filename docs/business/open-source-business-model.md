# Open Source Business Model

## Philosophy

The platform can be open source while the company earns revenue from services.

## Possible Revenue

- hosted cloud workspace
- team collaboration
- enterprise deployment
- migration services
- AI services
- support
- OEM / embedding

## Strategic Asset

The long-term moat is not only source code.

It is:

- ecosystem
- community
- format adoption
- integrations
- compatibility knowledge
- trust
- hosted services

## Licensing Direction

Possible future structure:

- open format specification
- permissive SDKs and adapters
- stronger copyleft for server/application layers
- protected trademark
- commercial licensing where appropriate

Final licensing decisions require dedicated review.
