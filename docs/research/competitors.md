# Competitive Landscape

## Positioning

Tachiko Work is not primarily competing as an Office clone. It targets a new category: semantic, Git-native, AI-native documents and computational workspaces.

## Existing categories

### Microsoft Office

Strength:
- Massive adoption
- Industry standard formats
- Enterprise distribution

Weakness:
- Historical compatibility burden
- Limited Git-native workflows
- Documents are not designed as structured computational objects

### LibreOffice / ONLYOFFICE

Strength:
- Open source Office alternatives
- Strong document compatibility

Weakness:
- Primarily preserve the traditional Office model
- Do not fundamentally redefine documents as semantic data

### HackMD / Notion

Strength:
- Collaborative knowledge work
- Modern UX

Weakness:
- Limited Git-native workflows
- Documents and computation are not unified

### Grist / Baserow

Strength:
- Spreadsheet plus database concepts
- Structured data

Weakness:
- Not primarily focused on Git-native development workflows

### Game data tools

Examples:
- Charon
- Machinations
- Balruno

They validate the existence of the game-data problem, but Tachiko Work aims at a broader semantic data platform.

## Strategic opportunity

The market is fragmented:

- Office documents
- Markdown documents
- Spreadsheets
- Game data
- Computational notebooks
- AI workflows

Tachiko Work aims to unify these categories around a shared semantic model.
