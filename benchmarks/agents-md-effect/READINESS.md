# Readiness Verdict

Case construction is complete enough for audit: nine authentic cases and tasks
are frozen; all bases are root-`AGENTS.md`-free; bases, targets, replay relations,
and authority resolve; focused historical/rebased pilots pass; scoring is
assertion-level; and candidate-core validation is specified separately from
hidden overlays. This does not make the execution harness ready.

Formal Baseline A is blocked by defects that cannot be converted into candidate
scores:

1. no immutable provider deployment attestation for `gpt-5.6-sol`, and no
   provisioned sealed authentication plus account/project/entitlement receipt;
2. the isolated agent/validator/controller accounts are not provisioned or
   verified, including confinement of candidate-controlled build scripts/tests
   away from controller, source, other-run, arm-key, receipt material, and
   confidential/mutable oracle source; the fixed neutral runtime/account paths
   are not staged, hash-verified, or leak-scanned;
3. required solution-neutral adapter families and their positive/negative
   qualification are incomplete, the current historical oracle commands have
   no staged production command manifest compatible with oracle secrecy, and
   all nine target paths lack content-addressed positive plus behavior-missing/
   base-negative discrimination qualification;
4. no end-to-end semantic controller/result validator yet binds execution,
   assertion selection, points, caps, receipts, valid/invalid stage semantics,
   and schemas, including positive and adversarial record fixtures;
5. TW-05's package-manager-neutral offline environment is not qualified beyond
   pnpm;
6. the effective catalog/feature/MCP/prompt/isolation preflight is not yet one
   fail-closed per-result controller step; the all-nine construction-only base
   unions pass, but same-wave base controls are not integrated, and invoked
   Rust/Cargo/Git binaries plus WASM target artifacts are not all hash-verified;
7. candidate capture still trusts the agent-mutatable repository index, ignore
   rules, attributes, filters, and local configuration; a raw-filesystem,
   no-filter trusted capture plus adversarial qualification is missing;
8. eligible case-specific reviewer panels, attestations, and packet-only offline
   reviewer environments are specified but not provisioned or negatively
   qualified for a standalone Baseline case; the frozen packet-wide
   variant-text scan/redaction contract is also not implemented or fixture-
   qualified, and the byte-exact reviewer allocator lacks independent
   reference-vector replay/negative qualification;
9. case constructors saw Baseline A; neither the variant-blind intrinsic audit
   nor the independent post-freeze, A-aware/no-edit comparison audit has
   occurred.

No Baseline A task may run until all nine are closed and an independent
readiness review passes.
