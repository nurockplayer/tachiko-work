# Tachiko Work Product Roadmap

The repository-wide product roadmap lives at
[`docs/product/product-roadmap.md`](docs/product/product-roadmap.md).

That page is a planning and navigation projection over GitHub Issues and the
single product-stage Milestone axis established by Issue #56. It does not
supersede the Product Constitution, Accepted ADRs and policies, or normative
specifications.

The current planning horizon is `02 · Core & Format Hardening`. Milestones are
maturity and dependency horizons, not calendar commitments; no due dates are
implied.
